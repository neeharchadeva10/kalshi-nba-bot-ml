## Multiple-looks warning

This audit looked at 194 slices (about 25 assumptions, but several have many slices: 30 teams x 3, 30 home teams, ...). At 95% about 9.7 slices are expected to look significant by chance; 65 had a CI excluding 0 (many only because E1's overall mean residual is itself non-zero) and 42 differ from the overall bias. With a Bonferroni cut-off (alpha 0.00026, normal approximation to the bootstrap z) 15 survive (vs 0) and 6 survive (vs the overall bias). Slices overlap (a game is in a month, a team, a rest class, ...), so they are not independent. Any fix must be pre-registered and confirmed on NEW games (the forward tracker) before it counts.

## Notes

- OT flag: play-by-play where available (2024-25, 2025-26), else player-box minutes (2020-21+); 2019-20 has neither, so OT slices exclude it.
- Roster continuity (M3) uses only players who appeared for the team in strictly earlier games of the season (the opening-night roster is unknown), ranked within the same game number; available from 2021-22 (needs the previous season's player box). The first game has no value.
- M12 excludes 2019-20 (bubble). 'Far out' = 4+ wins behind the conference's 10th-best win total before the game (my definition; PLAN.md gives none). Trade-deadline dates are from memory and unverified.
- Time zones use a static table by home team; Phoenix = Mountain (-7), no DST, so the clock difference to Pacific is ignored in summer. Bubble games are treated as Orlando (no shift computed), Cup semis/finals as Las Vegas.
- M14 uses usual starters defined after the fact (top 5 by starts in the season); 2020-21+ only.
- Slice bootstrap CIs are for the mean residual (game resampling, 2,000 draws, seed 20261023); margin CIs for game slices are in slices.csv.
