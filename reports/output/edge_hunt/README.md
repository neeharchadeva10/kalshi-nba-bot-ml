# Edge hunt: results (2025 NFL game winners)

*Generated data is in this folder (`kpm edge-hunt`). Each idea was chosen on weeks 1–9 and judged on weeks
10 onward plus the playoffs. Orders are 100 contracts with fees included. The fill checks use Kalshi's own
trade prints.*

| # | Idea | Result | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kalshi-only arbitrage: buy both teams' YES (or both NO) for under $1 after fees | Positive in 47 of 2.8M game-minutes; 1 fillable trade all season (+0.4¢) | Doesn't exist |
| 2 | Predictable drift into kickoff (favorites, underdogs, momentum, reversal; 1–120h out) | 3 of 24 rule sets positive on early weeks. The best (bet against the side that moved, 3h out, hold) went +5.5¢ → **−6.5¢** on later weeks | Failed |
| 3 | Price buckets (favorite-longshot bias) | Best early bucket (teams at 20–40¢, 24h out): +4.6¢ → +9.5¢ on later weeks, with intervals spanning zero, out of 15 combinations tried. The 20-season sportsbook check found no persistent bias: 20–40% teams won 30–31% at a 31% price in 2011–2024, so 2025 (+5.5 points) was a one-season blip | Noise |
| 4 | Better odds: buy from whichever of Kalshi or Polymarket US is cheaper (all-in) | Polymarket US was cheaper 60–66% of the time, saving about 0.25¢ per contract on average | Small saving |
| 5 | Better odds: resting limit bid on Kalshi instead of paying the ask | Filled 57–76% of the time within 30 minutes when placed 1–3h before kickoff, saving about 2.1¢ when filled. Fills lean toward worse outcomes, so the net gain is about 0.2–1¢ per contract | Small saving |

**Bottom line:** no edge in NFL game winners from public price data. Limit orders and venue shopping cut
costs a little, but an average bet still loses about 1–2¢ per contract to spread and fees.
