# BTC 15-minute markets: trend scan

*Produced by `kpm crypto-trends`. Rules buy at the ask (100 contracts, Kalshi taker fee) and hold to
settlement, one trade per market. Rules were chosen on June 2026 (at least 200 trades and a positive average)
and judged on July and on fresh Aug 5 – Oct 3 data. 94 rules were tested.*

**Strict verdict:** none of the 10 rules picked on June had a confidence interval above zero in both July and
Aug–Oct. Nothing is proven.

**Best lead: buy the favorite when it is priced 90–95¢ with 4–7 minutes left.**

| Period | Trades | Profit per contract | 95% interval | Favorite's win rate minus price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| June | 1,043 | +0.8¢ | n/a (chosen here) | +1.3 points |
| July | 1,181 | +1.0¢ | −0.5 to +2.3 | +1.5 points |
| Aug–Oct | 1,930 | +1.5¢ | +0.4 to +2.5 | +2.0 points |

Nearby settings (80–95¢ favorites, 4–10 minutes left) also lean positive in all three periods. This fits a
longshot bias near expiry. The risk is a −90¢-plus loss whenever the favorite flips.

**Reliable losers (outside June, with intervals below zero):**

| Rule | July | Aug–Oct |
|---|---|---|
| Buy either side at 50–60¢ with 10–14 minutes left | −2.6¢ | −2.2¢ |
| Buy the underdog at 40–50¢ with 4–7 minutes left | −3.3¢ | −3.9¢ |

These lose because near 50¢ the fee is at its maximum, and with the spread that's about 2¢ of cost and no
mispricing to recover it.

**No pattern found:** following or fading BTC momentum, or time of day (the average spread is about 0.8¢ in
every session).
