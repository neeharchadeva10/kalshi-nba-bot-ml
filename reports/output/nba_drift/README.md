# Research log #24 results: pre-game drift (NBA primary, NFL secondary)

Generated by drift.py per PREREGISTRATION.md. Interpretation choices:

- Quote validity: a candle side counts as missing when bid <= 0 or ask >= 1 or bid > ask (repo clean_quote).
- Exclusions are per game and per entry time: a game is skipped at E when EITHER team market has no valid bid/ask at the entry candle or at the close candle (so the side choice never depends on a missing quote).
- A candle is chosen first (last one closing at or before E / T-5min), then tested for validity; no staleness limit.
- V1/V2 pick by entry mid vs 0.5 (V1: higher-mid team if > 0.5; V2: lower-mid team if < 0.5; a 0.5/0.5 game is skipped).
- V5/V6: change = mid(E) - mid(last candle at or before E-24h), per team; V5 buys the team with the larger positive change, V6 the team with the larger negative change; skipped if either history mid is missing or the change is 0.
- V7: edge = Elo probability - entry mid; buy the team with edge >= 0.03 (larger edge if both). Elo updates on every game with a known result in tip order (excluded-for-quotes games still update Elo); warm-up games are not traded.
- NFL Elo and warm-up restart per season (1500 at each season start; first 2 weeks of each season are warm-up).
- Split: all games with a clean result, sorted by tip; first ceil(N/2) = discovery, the rest = unseen (same split for every version).
- Bootstrap CI: percentile 2.5 / 97.5 of the mean over 10,000 resamples of whole games (one trade per game); the rule-2 bound is the 2.5th percentile.
- Coin flip: 500 copies, each buys a uniformly random team of the same unseen games the strategy traded; share of copies with mean CLV net >= the strategy's.
- $/week = unseen mean CLV net x 10 x (unseen trades / unseen weeks); unseen weeks = ISO weeks with at least one unseen game (amendment 2026-10-20).
- NBA: occurrence_datetime is missing on most historical markets; expected_expiration_time (equal to occurrence_datetime wherever both exist) is used instead. Hourly candles were fetched from max(open, tip-50h) so the T-24h entry has its 24h lookback.
