# Public evidence finds no proven free-data edge

**Neither NBA thin markets nor Kalshi daily temperature has a public, audited, after-cost edge that a small trader on free official data can copy.** The best-documented effects are real but small or not tradable at retail cost: Kalshi NBA live prices underreact to game events (pass-through of only about 0.64), yet the authors' own executable 5-minute returns stay negative after the spread (-0.9 cents per contract at gaps of 200 bp or more) ([Angelini & De Angelis](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.07811)). Player props, team totals and quarter/half lines have no rigorous efficiency study at all, so any edge there would have to be measured from scratch. Injury-news timing is a seconds-scale race against sportsbooks, which free feeds cannot win. For weather, the August 2026 switch to The Weather Company (TWC) has broken the old NWS-CLI replication pipeline and no one has published how TWC derives its value, but that gap is a risk and a research question, not an edge. The one structural result with real Kalshi data behind it is the favorite-longshot bias: contracts at 10c or less lose over 60% after fees, while makers buying above 50c earned a small +2.6% (2021 to April 2025) ([Bürgi, Deng & Whelan](https://www.karlwhelan.com/Papers/Kalshi.pdf)). That result matches your repo's record of failed tests. The funnel should therefore spend its limited tests on a few cheap, pre-registered, structural rules and on forward data collection, with a strong prior that most will fail.

## Live NBA prices drift, but the spread eats the drift

The strongest recent NBA evidence uses 1,438 Kalshi games (2,876 contracts, 409,512 contract-minutes, April 2025 to May 2026). A 1-minute change in an out-of-sample win-probability benchmark moves the Kalshi midpoint only **0.64** as much (SE 0.010), and the unpriced remainder predicts further drift: a 10 percentage point gap predicts about **2.0 pp raw and 4.6 pp net drift at 5 minutes** ([Angelini & De Angelis](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.07811)). Underreaction is strongest in clutch time (pass-through 0.509) and is larger for lead changes in illiquid states. But two details cap its value. The benchmark has the same Brier score as the market (0.164 each), so it is not better information, and a buy-the-gap, hold-5-minutes rule earns midpoint returns of only +0.4 to +0.9 cents while executable returns (buy ask, sell bid) are -1.2 cents overall and -0.9 cents even at gaps of 200 bp or more. The high-quality-quote subsample is still -0.36 cents. No Kalshi fee is modelled, so the real result is worse. The paper does not test a maker-side entry, which is the only untested variant that could flip the sign. This also matches your own live NFL/NBA losses.

A second 2026 paper (23 million NBA/MLB/NHL moneyline trades, March to May 2026) finds calibration "visually indistinguishable from the diagonal" from 30 minutes to 4 hours before close, but a step-like distortion in the last 10 minutes (Prelec curvature above 1; NBA the least distorted league, Platt slope 1.62) ([Moshrefi](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14430)). My arithmetic on its pooled parameters (gamma about 1.3) puts a 90c favorite at about 94.6% true probability, roughly 4 to 5 cents before fees, but this is an inference and not a reported number. It may simply reflect trailing teams being priced off known game state, and the paper does no fee or PnL analysis. Its parlay result is the cleanest realized edge: parlays at 0.30 win about 24% and median overpricing grows from 0.99 at two legs to 1.22 at ten, but that favors selling parlay YES, which a taker cannot do cheaply, and parlays are a separate fee class (Kalshi added a combo market-maker fee in August 2026) ([Bitcoin.com / Citizens JMP](https://news.bitcoin.com/kalshi-beats-the-books-on-nfl-totals-loses-to-fanduel-on-moneylines/)).

## Props, totals and quarter lines are untested rather than proven soft

I found no peer-reviewed study of NBA player-prop efficiency, alt-line ladders, quarter lines, half lines or team totals, so claims of prop softness rest on practitioner blogs and vendor marketing. The academic record covers full-game totals only, and it is old: over 10,325 games (2000-01 to 2007-08) overs won 49.87%, a fair bet with no significant over-level pattern ([The Sport Journal](https://thesportjournal.org/article/nba-gambling-inefficiencies-a-second-look/)). The best-documented anomaly is early-season totals bias, with the under hitting 58.2% in week one in 2009-2012 data ([UWF](https://ircommons.uwf.edu/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/Early-season-NBA-over-under-bias/99380090347306600)) and a 56.72% against-the-close rate in Baryla et al. ([ETSU](https://dc.etsu.edu/etsu-works/17780)). Both predate 2018 and neither shows sample size or vig treatment. Pregame line momentum (Moskowitz, 2021) "fail[s] to overcome transactions costs" ([NBER](https://conference.nber.org/conf_papers/f92072/f92072.pdf)). Team totals are mechanically (game total plus or minus spread)/2 per forum-level sources, so a consistency check is cheap, but nobody has measured violation rates.

The prediction-market side is bleak for a small taker. Polymarket NBA player-prop markets averaged only 253 USDC of volume and 13.3 trades each, with median pregame spreads of 392 bps and 1,031 bps in-game ([arXiv 2605.00864](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.00864v1)). Kalshi prop depth is unpublished, and on Kalshi the maker side is almost always a professional firm ([InGame](https://ingame.com/susquehanna-biggest-sports-kalshi-volume-knicks)). Taker fees follow ceil(0.07 x C x P x (1-P)), about 1.75c on a 50c contract ([Rotowire](https://www.rotowire.com/prediction-markets/trading-fees)); the sports maker fee is unverified. Institutions regard Kalshi sports as lower-prestige, so thin props and brackets are where pros are least present, but depth is also shallowest ([eFinancialCareers](https://www.efinancialcareers.com/news/electronic-market-makers-trading-on-kalshi-are-small-fish-in-a-big-pond)). Citizens JMP puts Kalshi's NFL totals vig at 4.50% before fees against 4.80% at FanDuel, which suggests that pre-fee pricing is competitive with books ([Bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/kalshi-beats-the-books-on-nfl-totals-loses-to-fanduel-on-moneylines/)). The only genuinely free NBA data are play-by-play and box scores ([nba_data](https://github.com/shufinskiy/nba_data)); historical quarter, half and alt-line odds are not free, so Kalshi's own candlestick and trade history, plus forward collection, is the only realistic price source.

## Injury news is a race that free feeds lose

The official injury report is free and timestamped, due by 5 pm local the day before, with the game-day report between 11 am and 1 pm local (1 pm for the second night of a back-to-back), and starting lineups due 30 minutes before tip but amendable ([NBA Official](https://official.nba.com/nba-injury-report-2025-26-season/); [casino.org](https://www.casino.org/news/nba-could-change-lineup-submission-time-helping-bettors)). Teams are fined for misreporting ("Out" players who then played: 76ers $100,000, Magic $25,000) ([ESPN](https://africa.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/47190324/76ers-fined-injury-report-violation-regarding-joel-embiid); [NBA.com](https://www.nba.com/news/magic-fined-25k-violating-league-injury-reporting-rules)). After the October 2025 federal betting cases the league moved toward faster updates and wider surveillance ([Covers](https://www.covers.com/industry/nba-to-review-injury-policies-integrity-monitoring-after-betting-scandal-oct-28-2025)), so the real timing edge, nonpublic information, is now both illegal and monitored. Books reprice "in seconds, not minutes" (an unmeasured industry claim) ([Action Network](https://www.actionnetwork.com/education/how-injuries-affect-betting-lines-a-guide-to-market-movement)), and Kalshi's correlation with sportsbooks reaches 0.996 with a 2.75% cost threshold leaving few gaps ([Blockworks](https://app.blockworksresearch.com/research/from-betting-to-trading-how-kalshi-is-reshaping-sports-markets)). Dare, Dennis & Paul found pricing errors in opening lines with absent players but no profit at the close ([RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finlet/v13y2015icp130-136.html)), and an informal 42-star check found 49.1% ATS with the star versus 51.3% without ([Action Network](https://www.actionnetwork.com/nba/nba-betting-picks-odds-angles-matt-moore-november-2-2019)). I found no measurement of how often "questionable" players play, how often status changes after the deadline, or how fast Kalshi moves after a report. The prop-lag claim (props move after spreads, around 1:30, 5:30 and 8:30 ET) is a single blog with a self-reported, unverifiable 30% ROI ([amicsta](https://amicsta.substack.com/p/how-to-beat-nba-props)). Because those are all things a trader can archive for free (PDF snapshots against box scores and Kalshi quotes), they are research targets, but the expectation is that Kalshi follows the books within seconds.

## Temperature markets: the settlement source just changed under everyone's models

Pre-switch, Kalshi daily highs settled on the NWS Climatological Report (CLI), which comes from ASOS's internal whole-degF rolling 5-minute average, not from the public 5-minute METARs (whole degC, so 1 to 2F off) or hourly METARs (tenths degC) ([NWS Chicago](https://www.weather.gov/lot/weather_observations_faq); [NWS LOX](https://www.weather.gov/lox/asostemperature)). The hourly METAR T-group maximum is therefore a floor on the CLI high, and the gap when a peak falls between hourly reports is unquantified. The climate day is local standard time year-round, so in daylight time the day runs 1:00 AM to 12:59 AM ([Kalshi Help](https://help.kalshi.com/markets/popular-markets/weather-markets)). Daily highs and lows moved to TWC at about August 27, 2026 (one source says August 14), with stations still named by CLI IDs (NYC Central Park, Chicago Midway, LAX); live rules say "according to The Weather Company," the weather.com/kalshi "Final" is "provided for convenience only," and the rulebook reserves the right to change the source ([TurbineFi](https://www.turbinefi.com/blog/how-to-trade-kalshi-weather-markets-2026); [Artemis](https://artemis.bm/news/prediction-market-kalshi-partners-with-the-weather-company-for-weather-hedge-settlements)). Kalshi's own earlier TWC hourly rules warn that preliminary readings "may differ from the final reported value due to rounding and conversion" ([Kalshi Help](https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/13823837-weather-markets)). Nobody has published TWC's derivation, sensor, day window or any mismatch rate, so every pre-August NWS/IEM backtest has an unknown settlement-definition break. Bracket semantics add model risk: a 2F-inclusive B-bracket mis-read as 1F gave a phantom +1640% edge, and UTC-versus-standard-day aggregation cost about 14 pp of accuracy (third-party skill, unverified) ([claude-trading-skills](https://claudeskills.info/skills/agiprolabs/claude-trading-skills/kalshi-weather-markets/)).

On edges, the evidence is negative or marketing. A February 2026 bot lost 0-32 with Gaussian errors that underestimated tails (2-sigma events about 10 to 12% versus 5%) and flat fees that ruin cheap contracts (1c on 5c is 20%) ([MAXIMUS](https://www.northlakelabs.com/max/blog/kalshi-weather-postmortem-and-pivot/)). Liquid NYC prices reportedly already track the public NWS forecast ([BotForKalshi](https://www.botforkalshi.com/blog/can-you-bet-on-the-weather)). A vendor backtest (22 of 50 "Hot Forecast Breakout" variants profitable, 0 of 50 contrarian) is self-described as "directional, not production-grade" ([TurbineFi](https://www.turbinefi.com/blog/how-to-trade-kalshi-weather-markets-2026)), and win-rate claims of 67% to 68.5% are unverified ads. Your own weather test #2 (long-shot bias) already failed. What survives as defensible niches are same-day pricing against the observed running maximum in late afternoon (blog opinion that it is "closer to arbitrage than forecasting") and a maker version of longshot fading, both uncosted. The Kalshi LP program lists "Daily High/Low for All Listed Cities" as incentivized, but it needs 100+ contracts on both sides to score and carries two-sided inventory risk ([Kalshi LIP](https://help.kalshi.com/incentive-programs/liquidity-incentive-program)).

## A pre-registered funnel: five testable rules and the data each needs

The macro prior is unfriendly: Kalshi retail takers lost an estimated $583.5M (July 2021 to May 2026), two-thirds in sports, and Kalshi itself reports 2.9 losers per winner, though the taker-equals-retail proxy is disputed ([Roosevelt Institute](https://rooseveltinstitute.org/blog/since-kalshis-launch-ordinary-users-have-lost-half-a-billion-dollars/)). Every rule below should be logged before testing with a frozen train/test split, exact fee formula (series-specific maker fees verified on the market page), fills only at prices that traded, quotes sized to the real $1-per-trade account, and a 500-copy random-entry baseline matched on trade count and exits. Because makers may have free resting fills only on some series, the rule list leans maker-side.

| # | Rule (pre-register) | Free data | Pass bar and kill condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Taker avoidance filter: never buy below 15c; test only prices of 50c or higher, maker-resting where fee-free | Kalshi trade/candle history | Beats coin-flip baseline net of fees on unseen months; the paper's +2.6% (SD 33% per contract) means thousands of trades are needed, so likely underpowered |
| 2 | NBA live drift, maker-only: post bid when benchmark gap is 200 bp or more, exit at 5 min | Kalshi 1-minute bid/ask, NBA play-by-play | Net positive after fees and with fills on actual trades only; midpoint ceiling is +0.9c, so fail if it needs spread crossing |
| 3 | NBA moneyline last-10-minutes favorites (price 0.70 to 0.92) | Kalshi trades with close time, final score | Edge of 2c or more net of fee and depth-limited fills; check whether it just proxies known game state |
| 4 | NBA early-season (Oct to Nov) totals under, re-tested on 2018 to 2026 | Free box scores plus Kalshi totals ladders (history is short) | Under rate above break-even net of fee; kill if the effect is gone post-2018 (pregame lines older than that are not free) |
| 5 | Weather: late-afternoon running-max versus bracket price in cities with 30+ days of TWC settlements | IEM hourly METAR T-group, TWC final on weather.com/kalshi, Kalshi bracket prices | First measure TWC-versus-CLI mismatch rate and CLI-minus-METAR gap per city; trade only after settlement is reproducible and net of fee; log station, climate day and bracket inclusivity before testing |

Two additional data-gathering jobs cost nothing and should start now, because no public data exists: archiving the 15-minute injury-report PDFs and Kalshi prop/ladder quotes around report timestamps, and logging Kalshi ladder and quarter/half quotes to test cross-market consistency (sum of quarters versus game total, ladder monotonicity). The Moshrefi and Angelini papers have unnamed data providers and no fee modelling, so replicating them in your own cached Kalshi history is also a test of whether their numbers hold at your size. Untested ideas from the sources (NOAA/NBM bias correction worth about 0.7F in one 2018 Colorado study) are unproven for settlement and should not be funneled until rule 5's settlement checks pass.

## Conclusion

The cleanest takeaway is how little of this is knowable from public sources. The two most recent and rigorous Kalshi-specific papers both stop short of a net-of-cost result, and one of them reports negative executable returns in its own appendix; everything else is blogs, vendor claims or pre-2018 sportsbook studies. The live drift and last-10-minute favorite effects are therefore best treated as hypotheses about maker-side fills, not signals to take.

The weather switch to TWC changes what counts as evidence: until TWC's derivation and the TWC-versus-CLI gap are measured, any strategy tuned on NWS history is partly untested. Collecting a few months of forward settlement data is likely worth more than another forecasting model. Given your repo's record, the most probable funnel outcome is that most rules fail the unseen test or the coin-flip comparison; the funnel's value is in confirming that cheaply, and in building the forward datasets (injury PDFs, prop quotes, TWC settlements) that the literature lacks.

**Verified against the primary sources:**

**Corrections and cautions for the funnel table:**
1. **Rule 3 (last 10 minutes):** Moshrefi measures time to the market's *close*, which a trader only knows afterwards. A testable rule must use the game clock from play-by-play (for example, Q4 with N minutes or less left), or it has look-ahead.
2. **Rule 5 (weather):** the table names Iowa Mesonet (IEM) METAR data. IEM is not an approved source in this project. Use NOAA sources only: NCEI ISD/ASOS, api.weather.gov observations, and the CLI/CF6 products. Step 1 (TWC vs NWS mismatch rate) is already running with NOAA data.
3. **Rule 4 (early-season unders):** Kalshi's KXNBATOTAL history covers only one early season (Oct-Nov 2025), so a historical test is underpowered. The current early season would have to be a forward test.
4. **For the running cheap-leg test (#33):** Bürgi et al. are a strong prior against it. Contracts at 10c or less lost more than 60% after fees in their sample.
5. **Fees:** the project has verified Kalshi's fee rounding to $0.0001 from real fills (not to the cent). The sports maker-fee status is still unverified and should be read from each series' `fee_type`.
